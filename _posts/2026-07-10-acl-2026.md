I went to ACL 2026! Me and and my co-author Filip travelled to San Diego to present our first ever publication (in case you have missed it, if you are into Mechanistic Interpretability and Multimodality, check [this](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22170) out!). And damn, I got so overwhelmed. 

Although I have shifted research with my upcoming PhD journey, meeting so many people into Mechanistic Interpretability and related fields really fills me with joy and, for some reason, also with that curiosity that I have missed a bit while trying to get on rails with my current reserch path. Computational Lingustics is such a fascinating broad reaserch area, with a growing interest even in a multimodality context! But yeah, to keep it short: great vibes, great people, for a conference that reminds me more than ever of how fun reserach can be.
